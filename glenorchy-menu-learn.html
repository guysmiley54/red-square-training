Dietary and allergens

Severe allergiesThe kitchen uses shared equipment, so traces of meat, seafood, dairy, gluten, nuts and other allergens may be present. Never promise a dish is allergen free. Always tell the kitchen about an allergy.
CodeMeaning
VVegetarian
G or GFGluten free as served
DFDairy free as served
GFOA gluten free option is available on request. Not gluten free as listed
DFOA dairy free option is available on request
NO ALTNo alterations. Served as listed, with no changes or dietary alternative
NUTS, SEEDSContains nuts or seeds
  • Nuts: the Mexican SMASH and Chilli SCRAM both carry a nuts warning because of the dukkah.
  • NO ALT dishes: Apple Crumble French Toast, Lamb Gyro and Chicken Gyro.
  • Coeliac wanting Eggs Benedict: it is GFO. Gluten free toast is a $3 swap, and you tell the kitchen.
  • Dairy free and Eggs Benedict: hollandaise is made with butter, and the dish is not marked DFO.
  • Vegetarian and dairy free lighter options: Spring Rolls and Chips. Wedges are DFO because of the sour cream.
  • Nachos: vegetarian and gluten free, but not dairy free.
  • Sweet Pancake: vegetarian only. No gluten free or dairy free option.
  • Vegan drink: the Mont Blanc.
  • Tree nut allergy: the Coconut Cold Brew Latte contains almond milk syrup.
